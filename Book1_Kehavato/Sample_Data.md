# Kehavato (Proverbs) Sample Data
1. **ઉતાવળે આંબા ન પાકે (Utavle aamba na paake)**
   * Meaning: Mangoes don't ripen in a hurry (Patience is a virtue).
2. **ટીપે ટીપે સરોવર ભરાય (Tipe tipe sarovar bharay)**
   * Meaning: Drop by drop, a lake is filled (Small, consistent efforts lead to big results).
3. **જેવું વાવો તેવું લણો (Jevu vavo tevu lano)**
   * Meaning: As you sow, so shall you reap.

## New Additions for Quiz & Flashcards

### Proverb 4
**Gujarati:** "લોભે લક્ષણ જાય"
**Pronunciation:** Lobhe lakshan jaay
**Literal Translation:** Greed makes one lose their virtues/character.
**Meaning:** Greed destroys good qualities; excessive desire leads to a person's downfall or moral compromise.
**Story/Context:** Originating from tales of merchants who lost everything trying to chase a little more profit, ignoring honesty.

### Proverb 5
**Gujarati:** "ખાડો ખોદે તે પડે"
**Pronunciation:** Khaado khode te pade
**Literal Translation:** He who digs a pit, falls into it.
**Meaning:** If you scheme or plot to harm others, you will ultimately bring ruin upon yourself.
**Story/Context:** A universal concept found across cultures, in Gujarati folklore often told through the story of a cunning fox trapping itself.
